# Findings Summary

Based on 96,470 delivered orders (Sep 2016 to Aug 2018) from 93,350 customers.
Total item revenue: 13,220,249 BRL. Freight: 2,198,146 BRL. Order value (items plus freight): 15,418,395 BRL.

## Findings
1. **Growth:** Jan-Aug 2018 revenue was 7,217,172 BRL vs 2,993,277 BRL in Jan-Aug 2017 (+141.1%). Monthly revenue peaked in Nov 2017 at 987,648 BRL (7,288 orders). Jan-Aug 2018 stayed between 826,437 and 977,545 BRL a month, so growth levelled off.
2. **Categories:** health_beauty (1,233,132 BRL), watches_gifts (1,165,899), and bed_bath_table (1,023,435) are the top three. Together they are 25.9% of revenue.
3. **Repeat rate:** 3.00% of customers (2,801 of 93,350) ordered more than once. Split: 97.0% one order, 2.76% two, 0.19% three, 0.05% four or more.
4. **Delivery and reviews:** 6,534 orders (6.8%) were late. Average review: 4.29 on time, 3.29 for 1-3 days late, 2.10 for 4-7 days, 1.70 for 8+ days. All late orders together average 2.27. 62.4% of late orders scored 1-2 stars vs 9.3% of on-time orders.
5. **States by revenue:** SP 38.3% (5,066,563 BRL), RJ 13.3%, MG 11.7%, RS 5.5%, PR 5.0%. SP averages 8.3 days to deliver with 4.5% late.
6. **States by late rate (200+ orders):** AL 21.4% (24.0 days), MA 17.4% (21.1 days), SE 15.2% (21.0 days), PI 13.9%, CE 13.8%.
7. **States by late order count:** SP 1,820, RJ 1,495 (12.1% late), MG 519, BA 396 (12.2%), RS 325. RJ alone is 22.9% of all late orders. AL, MA, and SE together are 261 (4.0%).
8. **RFM:** High-value one-time buyers are 21,566 customers (23.1%) and 54.7% of order value. Champions (repeat, recent) are 1,544 customers (1.7%) and 3.2% of order value. Loyal but lapsing are 1,257 (1.3%) and 2.5%.

## Caveats
- Associations, not causes: late deliveries and low scores move together, but other factors may play a part.
- High-value one-time buyers are defined as top-quartile spenders, so their large revenue share is partly built into the definition. The useful point is that a second purchase from this group would matter a lot.
- RFM revenue includes freight, so its total is higher than the item revenue above.
- 646 delivered orders have no review and are excluded from review averages only.
